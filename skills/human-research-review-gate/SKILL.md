# Human Research Review Gate

Ask for one explicit decision and preserve its exact reference. Cite the TODO, state, and evidence; separate facts, interpretation, predicate judgments, and recommendation.

## Requests

- **Lifecycle:** give the revision and every eligible rule, including self; recommend one and route the reply to `$research-state-transition`.
- **Parameter:** compare all candidates by the predefined metric, dispersion, coverage, failures, cost, and sensitivity; ask for approval, rerun, or redesign.
- **Figure:** present every draft with its question, source, statistical unit, method basis, conditions, sensitivities, and coverage; record requested retention, exclusion, revision, grouping, or schematic and the first-presentation milestone.
- **Scientific revision:** state affected meaning and evidence; request an in-place pre-figure revision or a post-presentation versioned TODO from the current implementation.
- **Run over one hour:** provide the validated command, working directory, resources, outputs, checkpoint/resume behavior, progress/log paths, and completion/failure criteria; ask the researcher to run it and return the outputs.

## Output

Return the decision type, references, verified evidence or eligible rules, recommendation, exact request, and response when available. Silence and result direction are not approval; never omit an eligible self-transition or figure condition.
