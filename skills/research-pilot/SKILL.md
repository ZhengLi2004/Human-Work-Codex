# Research Pilot

Produce enough controlled preliminary evidence to decide whether to revise implementation, diagnose a bounded issue, freeze Confirmation, proceed to figures, or stop.

## Stage outcome

Separate framework validation from refinement: first test the approved framework against the primary criteria and comparable baselines with minimal justified settings. Substantial tuning follows supporting evidence. If the framework fails, distinguish implementation defects from structural mismatch and recommend upstream revision before expanding the search; keep any change within the existing approval process. Guardrail violations require diagnosis, while in-range guardrails do not rank candidates.

Freeze the approved Pilot manifest before inspecting trends and verify a representative path through metrics, storage, aggregation, and recovery. Execute the approved manifest in that order under its repeat rule, adding `$research-batch-execution` when needed. If framework validation fails, record remaining refinement units as unexecuted with reasons and prepare the revision decision; preserve the complete planned grid. Reconcile the full grid through `$research-evidence-aggregation` and report dispersion, coverage, failures, and material costs.

Use `$research-statistical-analysis` when estimating uncertainty or proposing a confirmatory design. Keep Pilot-derived choices identifiable. Apply the TODO's parameter-selection and sensitivity criteria to complete evidence, then present the proposed value for researcher approval. An approved selection before figure presentation can update the TODO in place with its fingerprint synchronized; existing approval need not be requested again.

Record evidence and predicates and prepare every outgoing rule at the boundary.

## Debugging

Debug in place. Record the defect and stable IDs before replacing invalid artifacts, regenerate dependent indexes and tables, and preserve valid outcomes and the approved combination set.

## Output contract

Link the manifest, results, reconciliation, cost and sensitivity evidence. Explain the supported parameter recommendation, limitations, and prepared decision.

## Absolute rules

- Never add or remove combinations after inspecting results.
- Pilot evidence is not Confirmation evidence.
